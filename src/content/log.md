The research method is simple: watch a hundred videos in a genre, write down the same ten things
for each, and the genre tells you its rules. This is the sheet. Rows are appended; the genre pages
are rewritten from them.

Columns: **date** watched · **genre** · **link** · **format** · **promise** (kind, and the second
one if borrowed) · **hook** (the first line or frame, quoted) · **stakes** (what rides on it) ·
**payoff** (confirm / surprise / deferred / none, and what was paid) · **died at** (the second I
would have left, and why) · **note**.
